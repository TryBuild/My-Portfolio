About Me

Building Software with Purpose

I'm a motivated Software Engineer graduated from the University of Kurdistan Hewlêr (UKH), with expertise in mobile application development using Flutter and Dart, and front-end development with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. My work also spans systems-level programming in C++, Java, and SQL, with a strong interest in system design — architecting scalable, well-structured solutions from the ground up. I enjoy building software that is not just functional, but polished and user-centric.

I'm driven by problem-solving and take pride in writing clean, efficient code. I work with modern AI-assisted development workflows — including Vibe Coding with tools like Claude Code and Antigravity — which allows me to design, build, and iterate faster without compromising quality.

Outside of coding, I'm a car enthusiast — I love everything about automotive culture, from engineering to design. That same eye for detail and appreciation for craftsmanship carries through into every piece of software I build.
